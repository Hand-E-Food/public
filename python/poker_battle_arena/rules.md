# Poker Battle Arena

© 2026 Mark Richardson

## Setup

Each player needs a full poker deck, preferably with different back art to distinguish the two decks. Agree on how many jokers each deck should include. Place the Army Ranking list for the appropriate number of Jokers where both players can refer to it.

Place three pieces of A4/Letter paper between the players, short edges closest to the players. Draw two lines across each page, dividing it into three sections. Each page is a lane of the battlefield, and each section is a step of the lane.

Place a stack of poker chips beside the board for the players to share: 30x $1 chips and 6x $5 chips. Chips are unlimited, add more if you need to.

Shuffle your deck and place it face-down next to you. This is your draw pile. You will also have a retreat pile and a discard pile which start empty. The retreat pile is eventually reshuffled into a new draw pile. Cards in the discard pile are out of the game.

Choose a player to go first.

## Play

Each player will place armies of poker hands on the battlefield. Each army carries chips across the battlefield. When armies do combat, the winning army takes the losing army's chips. When an army crosses the battlefield and achieves conquest, the opponent must discard as many cards as the army has chips. The army then retreats and its chips are returned to the pile. When a player runs out of cards to discard, they lose the war.

There are five phases in each turn: draw, play, advance, combat, and conquest. After you have performed all five phases, it is your opponent's turn.

### 1. Draw

If you have less than five cards in your hand, draw from the top of your draw pile until you have five cards. If your draw pile is ever empty, immediately shuffle your retreat pile and place it face-down as a new draw pile. If your retreat pile is also empty, then draw as many cards as you can.

### 2. Play

Play between one and five cards face-down on your outside edge of one of the lanes. This is an army. You may look at your face-down armies at any time. The army must conform to one of the formations described in Armies below.

If the opponent has a face-up army in the same lane, do not add any chips to your army. When you can see all the cards, there is no gamble. Otherwise, when placing your army, place at least 1 chip on it as **ante**. You may **bet** an additional 1 or 2 chips. If there is a face-down opposing army, be aware you will need to immediately **call** and optionally **raise** in the Advance phase.

Unlike common Poker rules, you do not always play five cards. Only play the cards that fit the formation, even if it's just one card for a "Single" formation.

You may play one army in each lane, each turn, even if you have other armies in those lanes already on the battlefield. Keep your armies to the left side of the lane that it is in, to help distinguish which armies are yours or your opponent's.

If you have less than nine cards and you do not want to play an army, you may instead draw two cards. You should never have more than 10 cards in your hand.

### 3. Advance

If any of your armies is facing an opposing face-down army that has more chips than yours, you must either **call** the opposing army's bet, or **fold** and retreat. You **call** the opposing army's bet by adding chips to your army until it has the same number as the opposing army. You may also **raise** by adding 1 or 2 additional chips to intimidate your opponent. If you don't need to **call**, you cannot **raise**.

If you are not willing to add more chips to your army, you **fold**, causing your army to retreat. If they **fold**, give all of the army's chips to the opposing army, and place the army face-up in the retreat pile. If this causes another of your armies to face the opposing army, they too need to either **call** or **fold**.

Only consider your front-most army in each lane. Ignore armies behind them. Do not **call** or **fold** against a face-up army. When you can see all the cards, there is no gamble. 

After this, move each of your armies one step forward in their respective lanes. Start with your armies closest to your opponent. Armies in the final step will advance off the battlefield onto your opponent's outside edge.

### 4. Combat

If opposing armies are in the same step of the same lane, they fight. Turn both armies face-up. The highest ranked army wins (see Armies below.) If there is a draw, compare the highest card of each army, the winner being the higher of the two cards. If there is still a draw, compare the second highest card in each army, and so on. If the two armies come to an ultimate tie, both lose.

The highest individual card is an Ace, followed by King, Queen, Jack, and 10 down to 2. All suits are equal. In a "Full House", compare the triple cards, not the pairs.

The losing army retreats, gives its chips to the winning army, and is placed face-up in the retreat pile of the player owning the army. The winning army remains on the battlefield, face-up.

If the armies tie, they both retreat. Remove the chips from both armies and place the armies face-up on their respective retreat piles.

### 5. Conquest

An army that has past the last step of the battlefield damages the opponent. The opponent must discard as many cards as the army has chips (see Discarding Cards.) If you cannot discard enough cards, you immediately lose the game.

The successful army is placed face-up in the owning player's retreat pile.

## Discarding Cards

When you are damaged by a conquest, you must discard a number of cards. You may choose cards in your hand to discard, or discard cards directly from the top of your draw pile without looking at them. All discarded cards are placed face-down in your discard pile. If your draw pile is empty, shuffle your retreat pile to create a new draw pile. If your draw pile, retreat pile and hand are all empty and you still need to discard a card, you immediately lose the game.

## Armies

Each army must conform to one of the following formations. Each formation has a rank, with higher rank numbers being better. Jokers are wild cards and can be any suit and number you wish. The numbers in a "Straight" cannot wrap, i.e. an Ace is always the highest card and can not be used in a "Straight" with a 2, the lowest card.

|------|------------|-------------------------|
|   1  |  9         | Single                  |
|   2  |  ♥︎ ♥︎       | Flush of Two            |
|   3  |  9 8       | Adjacent Pair           |
|   4  |  9 9       | Pair                    |
|   5  |  ♥︎ ♥︎ ♥︎     | Flush of Three          |
|   6  | ♥︎9♥︎8       | Adjacent Flush          |
|   7  |  9 8 7     | Straight of Three       |
|   8  |  9 9 7 7   | Two Pair                |
|   9  |  ♥︎ ♥︎ ♥︎ ♥︎   | Flush of Four           |
|  10  |  9 8 7 6   | Straight of Four        |
|  11  |  9 9 9     | Three of a Kind         |
|  12  | ♥︎9♥︎8♥︎7     | Straight Flush of Three |
|  13  |  9 8 7 6 5 | Straight of Five        |
|  14  |  ♥︎ ♥︎ ♥︎ ♥︎ ♥︎ | Flush of Five           |

Note that "Five of a Kind" can only be achieved with a joker.

## Foul

If an army is revealed during combat or conquest, and it does not conform to one of the army formations, it is disqualified. It's chips are given to to the opposing army. The army is immediately placed in the discard pile, not the retreat pile, face-down. Additionally, the same number of cards are also discarded from the top of the player's draw pile. You may only discard from your hand if your draw pile and retreat pile are empty.

If the opponent's army was revealed and is a valid formation, it remains face-up despite this foul.
